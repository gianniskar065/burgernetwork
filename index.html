ABOUT BURGER NETWORK

Welcome to Burger Network!

Burger Network is a small Minecraft server inspired by the golden era of Minecraft. We love the old Minecraft atmosphere, simple gameplay and the feeling of discovering a world for the first time.

No unnecessary complications. Just Minecraft, creativity and fun.

Our goal is to bring back the feeling of classic Minecraft while creating new memories along the way.
